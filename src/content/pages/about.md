I'm Sakina. I make small tools for things that bug me. Most are command-line tools, a few are plugins for [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness), and now and then there's a Minecraft mod. A lot of them exist because some site I use every week has no API, or because a quick script got important enough that it needed tests.

The posts here are mostly about bugs I hit while building them. The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/soffits), and the [projects page](/projects/) lists what I'm working on.
